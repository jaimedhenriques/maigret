# Vetra sales kit

These are editable drafts for founder-led discovery and paid pilots. No messages have been sent. Vetra is a working name pending brand clearance. Confirm one launch country, supported check scope and the operational gates in [the launch plan](LAUNCH-PLAN.md) before promising real verification.

## Positioning

**One sentence:** Vetra helps hiring teams collect candidate-approved information, review sourced evidence and resolve discrepancies in one clear workflow.

**Buyer pitch:** “Your team should know which checks are waiting on a candidate, a source or a reviewer. Vetra makes that visible and gives candidates a clear place to participate and correct information. We are inviting three teams to help shape a scoped paid pilot.”

**Current status:** “The workspace and candidate portal work. An identity-provider adapter is implemented and requires activation. Employment, education and other background-data integrations need contracted sources. This is a pilot foundation, and we will state exactly which checks your pilot includes.”

Do not substitute “fully verified,” “fraud-free hiring,” “GDPR certified,” “enterprise ready” or “instant background checks” for the supported scope. “Review complete” means the case workflow is complete; it does not recommend a hiring decision.

## Fifteen-minute discovery call

1. How many hires and candidate checks do you run in a typical month, and in which countries?
2. Which roles require identity, employment, education or other checks, and who defines that scope?
3. Walk through the last completed check: how many emails, tools and minutes of HR work did it take?
4. Which provider do you use, what does it cost, and what causes delays or unresolved discrepancies?
5. How do candidates see their check scope, correct a mistake or get help?
6. Who owns the budget, privacy review, procurement and rollout?
7. Would a 30-day pilot with up to 20 workflow cases be worth €299 plus quoted provider costs if it reduces administration? What outcome would justify renewal?

Record operational facts and buying requirements. Ask for synthetic examples instead of a real applicant's personal data during discovery. Qualify out buyers who require unconnected check types immediately or expect an automatic hiring recommendation.

## Eight-minute demonstration

| Time | Show | Explain |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 min | Fictional case queue and filters | The reviewer can see scope, status and next action |
| 1–2 min | New invitation with explicit check scope | The employer requests only the agreed information; link delivery is currently manual |
| 2–4 min | Private candidate portal | The candidate sees scope, approves participation and submits statements; these remain statements until checked |
| 4–5 min | Manual evidence review | Record source and notes; show the “Manually reviewed” label and reviewer history |
| 5–6 min | Correction request and resolution | Explain discrepancies in plain language and let the candidate respond |
| 6–7 min | Identity check status and integration settings | Describe the adapter accurately; show real provider results only in a configured, supported environment |
| 7–8 min | Audit/export and withdrawal | Demonstrate the supported record and explain the agreed retention/provider cleanup process |

End with a concrete pilot outcome: measure coordinator minutes, candidate submission and evidence completion on the customer's supported check scope. Use only synthetic candidates in default demonstration mode.

## Pilot proposal template

> **Customer:** [legal entity and buyer]
>
> **Market and roles:** [one country and eligible role family]
>
> **Term:** 30 days from [date]. Two HR reviewers and up to 20 workflow cases. One case means one candidate invitation for an agreed screening request; corrections within the request are included.
>
> **Included:** candidate approval, scoped information collection, case tracking, source-based manual review, correction workflow, audit/export and weekly product feedback. [List separately any activated identity or contracted credential checks, provider, supported scope and evidence delivered.]
>
> **Excluded unless separately contracted:** criminal history, sanctions, credit, statutory right-to-work checks, multi-country screening, automated hiring decisions and checks without an activated provider.
>
> **Price hypothesis:** €299 workflow subscription plus applicable tax and provider charges quoted before submission. No candidate fees. No unagreed overages or automatic annual commitment. Quote local currency in the final contract.
>
> **Start conditions:** executed agreements and notices, defined processing roles/lawful basis, supported provider accounts, private authenticated deployment, agreed retention and tested correction/deletion/recovery procedures. If these are incomplete, use synthetic workflow testing only.
>
> **Success measures:** agreed baseline coordinator minutes, candidate submission within three business days, completed supported evidence checks, unresolved discrepancies, support effort and willingness to renew. Targets are testable pilot assumptions, not guarantees.
>
> **Responsibilities:** the employer defines eligible role scope and makes employment decisions; Vetra operates the agreed workflow; the contracted provider performs its specified checks. Agree support contacts, candidate corrections, data handling and termination before launch.
>
> **End of pilot:** factual results report, renewal decision and agreed export/deletion process. No named testimonial or case study without explicit permission.

The template is commercial drafting material. Attach the actual customer agreement, data-processing terms and provider scope rather than treating this summary as a finished legal contract.

## Objections and honest answers

| Buyer question | Suggested answer |
|---|---|
| Why not stay with our screening vendor? | Keep the provider if it supplies the checks you need. Test whether Vetra reduces coordination work and gives candidates a clearer process. Integrations must be confirmed before contracting. |
| Does this verify someone's identity? | With the identity adapter activated, a supported provider performs the identity check and we record its outcome. A candidate statement or public profile match does not prove identity. |
| Can you check criminal history or right to work today? | Those providers are not connected. We can scope a future integration; the pilot agreement includes only activated checks. |
| Is this compliant? | Compliance depends on country, role, check scope, contracts and operation. We document the supported process and evidence for your privacy/procurement review. We do not claim a blanket certification. |
| Can this reject candidates automatically? | The product reports evidence and discrepancies for human review. It does not score suitability or recommend hiring decisions. |
| Do you have SSO, an SLA or a certification? | The current pilot does not offer the full enterprise package. We will describe implemented controls and agree pilot support; enterprise commitments follow their release gates. |
| Can candidates withdraw or correct information? | The portal supports correction and withdrawal. We agree retention and provider cleanup obligations for your scope before processing real data. |

## Procurement materials to prepare

- A truthful capability matrix separating implemented, activated and planned checks.
- Selected-country check scope, provider list, subprocessors, hosting region and transfer arrangements.
- Customer terms, processing agreement, candidate notice, retention schedule and correction/support procedures.
- Security controls, access model, incident contact, backup/restore evidence and actual support commitments.
- Price sheet, definition of a workflow case, provider charges and billing/termination rules.
- A sample factual report with provenance and status explanations, using synthetic information.

The first customer conversation should validate the problem and budget. The first signed pilot should validate supported delivery. Enterprise promises come after the enterprise gates, not from a polished demonstration.
