# Candidate verification

Mode: Operate
Scope: private invitation approval, statements, hosted identity handoff, correction and withdrawal.
Audience: the invited candidate.
Job: understand the requested checks, decide whether to approve, and control participation.
Primary actions: approve or decline, submit scoped statements, resume an available identity session, request correction or withdraw.
Proof: requested scope, recorded approval and the candidate's own case/check status.
Constraints: private links, explicit consent, understandable provider availability, correction and withdrawal. The employer retains hiring decisions.

## Direction contract

This records the agent-selected, code-led direction; no concept-seed key or user-selected pixel comp is corroborated.

THESIS: Give candidate approval and rights enough context to support a clear, voluntary action.
OWN-WORLD: The workspace's warm-paper, graphite/forest Manrope system, expressed in a centered, open reading flow with bordered groups.
STORY: Understand purpose and scope → approve or decline → submit information → view progress and act on correction/withdrawal.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand and privacy link precede purpose, requesting organization, stage and requested scope; approval follows an explicit native checkbox in the invitation state.
FORM: Native labeled fields, checkbox, disclosures and buttons; cards and action groups stack on mobile. Loading, invalid/expired-link, error, saved and withdrawn states are explicit. No seed key was produced.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
