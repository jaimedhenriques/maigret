A few things to know before you start.

Is the early-access workspace free?

Yes. There is no subscription charge during early access, and no card is required to use the workspace. Your staff account is still required. Verification providers may apply their own usage charges once connected.

What can candidates see and control?

Candidates see the requested checks before approving them. They can provide statements, view check status, request a correction and withdraw consent. Withdrawal stops the workflow and revokes the invitation; any connected provider cleanup still needs confirmation.

Which checks can run today?

The pilot supports candidate statements and documented manual review of employment and education evidence. The hosted Stripe Identity integration needs activation before it can perform live checks. Criminal history and right-to-work providers are not connected.

Can we use it for international hiring?

International coverage is planned. Check availability, provider coverage and the lawful process differ by country. We are starting with a focused UK recruitment pilot and will validate each additional market before offering live screening there.

What is needed before a real candidate pilot?

Agree the hiring location and check package, candidate notices, provider agreements and retention rules. Complete identity provider activation and the operational, security and legal review for your intended use. The pilot readiness page keeps these requirements visible.
